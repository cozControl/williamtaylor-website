# DEMO-1B client Order guide

1. Log in as an authorized Order staff member and confirm the Demo Environment banner.
2. Open **Orders** from Admin navigation.
3. Search by Order number, customer name, email, or telephone; combine fulfilment, manual-payment, date, and sort filters as needed.
4. Open an Order and review the snapshotted customer, delivery, item, variant/SKU, quantity, and demonstration amounts.
5. For a new Order, use the offered **Confirmed** action. Statuses cannot be skipped.
6. Progress an eligible Order through **In Preparation**, **Ready**, **Dispatched**, and **Delivered**.
7. Add plain-text internal notes when authorized. Notes are immutable and never appear in customer-facing documents.
8. Cancel only an eligible Order and provide the required reason. Cancellation does not refund money or restore stock.
9. Open **Print Order Summary** for the protected customer-facing HTML summary.
10. Authorized staff may change the clearly labelled manual demonstration payment state with an internal reason.
11. A manually paid Order exposes **View Demo Receipt**. It is explicitly not a production tax receipt.
12. Review the immutable fulfilment and manual-payment timeline on the Order detail page.
13. Log out when the demonstration is complete.

The displayed prices are snapshots for demonstration. There is no authoritative Pricing engine, inventory reservation, payment gateway, settlement, accounting posting, shipping booking, fiscal receipt, or refund processing.

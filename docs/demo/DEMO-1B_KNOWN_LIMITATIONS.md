# DEMO-1B known limitations

- No public checkout
- No production Pricing engine, discount engine, tax engine, or currency conversion
- No Inventory reservation, stock deduction, or overselling guarantee
- No live payment gateway, callback, settlement, or accounting posting
- No real refunds; `refunded` is visible conceptually but unavailable
- No shipping-provider integration
- No production invoice numbering or tax/fiscal receipt
- No customer self-service Order dashboard
- No automated customer notifications
- HTML print views only; no new PDF dependency
- Demo Receipt is not a production tax receipt

Order Product/Variant names, SKUs, options, customer details, delivery details, quantities, and amounts are historical snapshots. Demonstration unit amounts are entered by authorized staff and are not authoritative catalogue prices.

The imported public homepage still emits its pre-existing legacy Base44 public-settings console error after logout. Admin Orders does not depend on Base44.

BE-6A.1 remains deferred. BE-6A.2 and DEMO-1C remain unstarted.

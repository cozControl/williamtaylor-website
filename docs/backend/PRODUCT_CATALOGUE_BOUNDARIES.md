# Product Catalogue Boundaries

BE-5A.1 is limited to six normalized catalogue tables and their transactional
domain actions.

It does not introduce Product permissions, policies, administration UI,
navigation, Factory v3, Product Media assignments, automatic Variant
generation, publication, dynamic public Product routing, Product APIs,
structured data, categories, collections, pricing, currency, discounts, tax,
inventory, warehouses, availability, cart, checkout, payments, orders, SEO,
customer images, AI, Cloudinary mutation, or irreversible deletion.

The five storefront Product routes remain explicit and static. `/about` remains
the only CMS Page projection. Factory v1, Factory v2, protected storefront
assets, and `willy` are preservation boundaries.

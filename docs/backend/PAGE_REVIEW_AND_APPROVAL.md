# Page Review and Approval

The Review queue supports title or slug search, page-type and state filters, allowlisted sorting, and pagination. Page detail distinguishes current draft, candidate, and designated published revision and includes readiness, typed comparison, previews, and immutable transition history.

Submission and changes requests require notes. Replacing an approved or scheduled candidate requires explicit confirmation and a reason. Saving a newer draft never replaces a candidate.

The code-owned policy currently permits self-approval for `standard` and `landing`. The policy contract supports future types that prohibit self-approval. Readiness is rechecked at approval and publication.
